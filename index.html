About

I'm a Technical Safety Engineer with over 40 years of experience across oil & gas, petrochemicals, refineries, and chemical manufacturing, spanning FEED, EPC, commissioning/start-up, and operations. My core expertise is in QRA, physical effects and consequence modelling, HAZID/HAZOP/PHA support, facility siting, ALARP demonstration, and independent review of safety-study documentation.

I currently provide remote and hybrid technical-safety consulting. More recently, I've been building on this experience with AI/ML and agentic coding training through IISc, applying it to practical tools like the incident-learning assistant for HAZOP/PHA preparation featured below.
